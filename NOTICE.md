## Why not a permissive software license

The unit database is derived from MegaMek mm-data and MekBay, which are
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 themselves, and the rule summaries paraphrase published
BattleTech rulebooks for private play. A commercial use of this project was
never available to give away. One license for the whole repository says that
honestly, instead of promising something the rest of the material withholds.

to attribute it. [`THIRD-PARTY.md`](THIRD-PARTY.md) lists bundled third-party
code, which keeps its own terms.
