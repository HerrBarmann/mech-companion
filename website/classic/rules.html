From hit to wreck

  • Damage goes to the location’s armor first, the excess to the internal structure.
  • As soon as structure is hit: roll for a critical hit.
  • A location’s structure at 0 → location destroyed. A destroyed torso takes the arm with it.
  • Center torso, head or both legs gone → the ’Mech is finished.

How to calculate

  • Add the heat from movement + the heat of all weapons fired.
  • Subtract heat sinks (default 10; legs standing in water cool additionally).
  • Mark the result on the scale – the value stays until the next end phase.
Piloting rolls & falls 2D6 ≥ Piloting + mod.

When to roll?

  • Took 20+ damage in one phase (+1 on the roll)
  • Gyro, hip, leg or foot actuator hit (immediately on the hit)
  • Kicked, charged or hit by a Death From Above
  • Charged or performed a DFA yourself (the attacker rolls too)
  • Running with a damaged hip/gyro · jumping with a damaged leg actuator or gyro
  • Standing up from prone

Falling

  • Damage: tonnage ÷ 10 (round up) × (fall height + 1) – on level ground that is 1×. Spread over locations in groups of 5.
  • Facing after the fall: 1D6 – the new position then determines the hit table (front/side/rear).
  • Pilot: separate piloting roll, otherwise 1 pilot hit. Automatic if the ’Mech is immobile or the modified target number is above 12.
  • Prone: +1 to own attacks; torso and head weapons fire normally, only one arm may fire (the other props the ’Mech up); prone target: −2 from an adjacent hex, +1 from a distance.
  • Standing up: 2 MP + piloting roll (failure: stay down, MP lost).

Turn sequence every turn from the top
  1. 1 Initiative

    Both sides roll 2D6, reroll on a tie. The higher roll wins – and therefore acts last. That is the advantage: you see what the opponent does first.

  2. 2 movement

    The initiative loser moves one ’Mech, then the winner, alternating until all have moved. Mark the movement mode (walk / run / jump) with a token.

  3. 3 Weapon attacks

    The initiative loser declares all targets first. All attacks count as simultaneous: damage is resolved only after the declarations, so a destroyed ’Mech still shoots back.

  4. 4 Melee (physical attacks)

    Punch, kick, charge, Death From Above. Only against ’Mechs in the same or an adjacent hex.

  5. 5 Heat

    Add heat from movement and weapons fired, subtract heat sinks, read the effects off the heat scale.

  6. 6 End phase

    Piloting rolls for falls, consciousness checks, burning woods, prepare to stand up. Then the next turn begins.

Five beginner mistakes learned the hard way
  • Forgetting facing changes during movement – they cost 1 MP each.
  • Firing every weapon because you can. Heat is the real enemy.
  • Standing still in the open: movement is the cheapest armor bonus.
  • Ignoring woods. +2 per hex tips every duel.
  • Showing your rear – that is where the armor is thinnest.
