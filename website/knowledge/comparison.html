Reference · Classic ↔ Alpha Strike

System comparison

Same universe, two speeds. The table translates every mechanic in both directions – for switching there and back.

In short: Classic is the simulation – every weapon, every location, every heat sink counts individually. Alpha Strike condenses that onto one card and plays about three to four times faster. Classic shines with 1–4 ’Mechs per side; from a company upwards, Alpha Strike becomes the friend of the evening.

And here on the site?

Both systems share the same structure: the hangar or hangar (AS) for creating units from the database, then “Into battle” – the digital record sheet replaces paper and pencil, including attack dice, round counter and end phase. One ’Mech, two systems: simply create it in both hangars, the database knows both sets of values.
