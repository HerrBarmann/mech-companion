Rules baseline & changes

The rules pages are based on Total Warfare / BattleMech Manual (Classic) and Alpha Strike: Commander’s Edition – as of August 2026.

On 16 September 2026 the new BattleTech Core Rulebook arrives and replaces Total Warfare. The core mechanics stay, but around 170 rules change in detail. As soon as the book is here, the rules pages will be checked and updated – every change is logged here.
