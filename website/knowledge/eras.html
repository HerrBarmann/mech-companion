Reference · Timeline

Eras

Top to bottom = the history in game nights. Pick an era, read off factions and technology, look up the colour scheme in the faction browser.

Every era shapes who faces off, which technology is on the table and which colours fit. For painting: all factions mentioned are in the faction browser with recipes.

2571–2781

Star League era

The golden age: the Star League unites the Inner Sphere, the SLDF is the largest army in history. The Amaris coup destroys everything – General Kerensky leads the SLDF into exile (their descendants become the Clans).

Who fights: the SLDF, with the Great Houses in the background

Technology: the peak (Royal variants) – much of it is later lost as LosTech.

2781–3049

Succession Wars

Three centuries of war over Kerensky’s legacy. Technology decays, factories die, ’Mechs are handed down from generation to generation. The classic “3025” period: Atlas, Marauder, Warhammer – rough, scarce, iconic.

Who fights: Davion, Steiner, Kurita, Liao, Marik; mercenaries everywhere (Wolf’s Dragoons!)

Technology: introductory tech: standard weapons, hardly any extras – perfect for beginner games.

3049–3061

Clan Invasion

The Clans return: faster, stronger, with superior technology and a strict code of honour (zellbrigen). Only the Battle of Tukayyid (ComStar!) stops the advance. The classic duel setup: an IS lance (4) against a Clan Star (5) with less tonnage.

Who fights: Jade Falcon, Wolf, Ghost Bear, Smoke Jaguar against the Inner Sphere; ComStar

Technology: Clan tech (ER, pulse, double heat sinks, OmniMechs) against IS tech catching up.

3062–3067

FedCom Civil War

The giant realm of Davion and Steiner breaks apart in fratricidal war – Katherine against Victor. Ideal for “House against House” battles with modern IS equipment.

Who fights: Steiner against Davion, everyone against everyone

Technology: top-end IS tech: LB-X, Ultra AC, targeting computers, the first home-grown OmniMechs.

3067–3081

Jihad

The Word of Blake plunges the Inner Sphere into a fanatical war – weapons of mass destruction included. Dirty battles, unusual alliances.

Who fights: the Word of Blake against everyone; the coalition under Devlin Stone

Technology: experimental tech (TacOps era), Celestial OmniMechs, C3i.

3081–3150

Republic & Dark Age

Devlin Stone’s Republic of the Sphere enforces disarmament – until the HPG blackout (3132) cuts communication and everything breaks open again. ’Mechs are rarer, mixed lances with vehicles are normal.

Who fights: the Republic, resurgent Houses, Clan occupiers (Jade Falcons, Wolves)

Technology: retro meets high tech: IndustrialMech conversions next to the most modern designs.

3151–today

ilClan era

The Wolves take Terra and proclaim the ilClan – the present of the storyline. Jade Falcons under Malvina Hazen, reborn Star League dreams, everything in flux. Our database reaches up to here.

Who fights: the Wolves on Terra, the Jade Falcons, all Great Houses reshuffled

Technology: everything on the table – from LosTech antiques to the latest generation.
