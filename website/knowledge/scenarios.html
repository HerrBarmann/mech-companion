Scenarios

Pick a scenario, enter points and round limit in the battle, play. All four work in both systems.

Four scenarios that work without preparation – for Classic and Alpha Strike. The point budget (BV or PV) and round limit can be entered directly when setting up the battle; the end phase checklist counts the rounds. For all of them: Forced Withdrawal – crippled units (torso structure exposed, weapons largely destroyed) withdraw over their own edge instead of standing to the last scrap of metal.

Scenario 1

Stand-Up Fight Standgefecht

Setup & deployment: equal points per side, deployment on opposite edges at least 24″ / 12 hexes apart. Don’t forget terrain in the middle – without cover it turns into a dice lottery.

Victory: whoever first destroys all enemy units or forces them to withdraw. With a round limit: more surviving points win.

Recommendation: 300–350 PV or 5,000–6,000 BV per side · round limit 10–12 or open.

The bread-and-butter scenario for learning the rules and testing lists.

Scenario 2

King of the Hill Geländeeroberung

Setup & deployment: one marker (coin, die, building) exactly in the centre of the table. Deployment as in the stand-up fight. Alternatively three markers along the centre line for more movement.

Victory: at the end of every round from round 3 on: one point per own unit within 6″ / 3 hexes of the marker. When the round limit ends, the side with more points wins – kills count for nothing here!

Recommendation: 250–300 PV or 4,000–5,000 BV · round limit 8–10 (do set one).

Rewards fast ’Mechs and punishes campers – the TMM king finally gets to shine.

Scenario 3

Breakthrough Durchbruch

Setup & deployment: the attacker (full points) starts on one edge, the defender (75 % of the points) deploys up to the centre of the table. The attacker wants to escape over the opposite edge.

Victory: the attacker scores every unit that escaped over the target edge at its point value; the defender scores every destroyed one. More points win.

Recommendation: attacker 300 PV / 5,000 BV, defender 75 % of that · round limit 10 – whoever is still on the table when the limit ends counts for nobody.

Asymmetric and fast – good when one side has stronger models: just assign the roles accordingly.

Scenario 4

Fighting Withdrawal Rückzugsgefecht

Setup & deployment: like the stand-up fight, but one side (the withdrawing side, 100 % points) must move at least one unit towards its own edge every round from round 4 on and exit there; the pursuer (120 % points) follows up.

Victory: the withdrawing side scores exited units in full, damaged exited units at half. The pursuer scores what was destroyed. More points win.

Recommendation: withdrawing side 250 PV / 4,000 BV, pursuer ×1.2 · round limit 10.

The high art: fighting backwards without letting the line break. Rear armor discipline pays off here.

Rules baseline: Total Warfare / BattleMech Manual · Alpha Strike: Commander’s Edition · August 2026.
