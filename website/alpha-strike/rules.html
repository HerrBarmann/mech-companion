Damage & critical hits two rows of bubbles

Procedure

  • Damage = the value for the matching range band (S, M or L).
  • 0* (minimal damage): attack allowed, to-hit +1 – on success exactly 1 damage.
  • Hits from behind: +1 damage. Front or rear is determined with a ruler from base centre to base centre.
  • Tick off armor first, then structure.
  • Every hit that damages structure: roll once on the crit table.
  • Structure empty or damage left over → ’Mech destroyed.

Vehicles, infantry & ProtoMechs what works differently

Basics

  • Heat only applies to ’Mechs (and aerospace units). Vehicles, infantry, battle armor and ProtoMechs have neither a heat level nor OV.
  • Infantry and battle armor never suffer critical hits – damage simply goes to armor and structure.
  • Vehicles also roll on the motive table on every hit (even pure armor damage); critical hits only on structure damage, as usual.
  • An immobile target (crew stunned, motive 12+) is attacked with TMM −4.

Heat & overheat only four levels

Effects — from the next round on

Attacks+ heat level
movement−2″ per heat level
From level 2TMM −1
Level 4 = “S”Shutdown
  • When jumping, heat penalties to MV and TMM do not apply.
  • Shut down means: do nothing for a full round, TMM −4, then heat back to 0.

Using overheat

  • Declare before the roll how many OV points you use.
  • Each point = +1 damage at short and medium range, but +1 heat in the end phase.
  • At long range only with the OVL ability.

Cooling down in the end phase

  • Add the overheat points used.
  • In water: −1 level.
  • Did not fire at all this round: heat drops all the way to 0.

Where to start?

Alpha Strike is the easier entry: one card per ’Mech, no hit locations, no bookkeeping of individual weapons. When the decisions get too coarse for you – which weapon, which location, exactly how much heat – you switch to Classic BattleTech. The same models work in both systems.

Turn sequence four phases
  1. 1 Initiative

    Both roll 2D6, reroll on a tie. The winner acts last and thus reacts to the opponent.

  2. 2 movement

    The loser moves one ’Mech, then the winner, alternating. With unequal numbers, the larger side moves proportionally more models per pass.

  3. 3 Combat

    Unlike movement: the initiative loser makes all of their attacks, then the winner. Every ’Mech attacks once. Damage is ticked off immediately, but its effects only apply in the end phase.

  4. 4 End phase

    Apply damage effects, remove wrecks, apply heat or cool down, check victory conditions.
